Who We Are

United Voluntary Youth Council, Imphal

The United Voluntary Youth Council (UVYC), Imphal was established and registered on 22 January 1984. The organisation has long worked in the field of youth development, social service and community advancement in Manipur.

Over the years, UVYC has built experience in voluntary action by promoting education, encouraging constructive youth participation, supporting cultural activities and responding to community needs with dedication and service.
