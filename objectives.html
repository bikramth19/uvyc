Building a Progressive and Inclusive Manipur

Step 01

Empower Youth

Develop leadership, confidence, discipline and social responsibility among young people.

Step 02

Support Education

Promote education, learning opportunities and awareness-building among students and youth.

Step 03

Serve Community

Undertake voluntary community service and locally relevant welfare initiatives.

Step 04

Preserve Culture

Celebrate and protect cultural identity, traditions and positive local values.

Step 05

Build Inclusive Society

Strengthen cooperation and inclusive development for a resilient and forward-looking community.

Working for youth development, social service, education, cultural preservation and community well-being in Manipur.
